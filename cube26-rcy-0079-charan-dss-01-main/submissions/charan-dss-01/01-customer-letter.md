# 01 · Customer Letter

**To:** Chief Operating Officer & Head of Marketplace Finance  
**From:** Charan, Recovery Manager Lead  
**Subject:** Eliminating Undue Marketplace Fees with Proof, Not Hope  

Dear Partner,

Every month, your marketplace channel deducts thousands of dollars in inbound defect fees, alleged missing inventory, unreturned customer refunds, and weight-tier adjustments. Today, your finance team either writes these off as an unavoidable cost of doing business, or hands a CSV to a recovery agency that claims a 25% contingency fee to fire off generic, ungrounded disputes.

When an agency disputes a charge without proof, your channel account standing suffers. When Amazon flags repeated unsubstantiated claims, they don't just deny reimbursement—they restrict your inbound shipping privileges and demote your seller tier.

**The root problem has never been accounting. It has always been the evidence disconnect.**

When your prep warehouse seals a polybag, covers a barcode with an FNSKU, and validates carton counts at receiving, a human makes a quick judgment that nobody ties to the financial ledger. Six weeks later, when the marketplace assesses an "inbound packaging defect fee" of \$38 per carton, you have no automated way to connect that specific fee back to the physical prep log proving compliance.

**Recovery Manager bridges that gap.**

Recovery Manager is not a generic chatbot. It has no camera, runs no vision models, and hallucinates nothing. Instead, it reads the structured operational logs produced by your Receiving, Prep, Pack, and Returns teams, matches each financial fee back through the physical unit journey, and makes a conservative, defensible judgment:

1. **CONTRADICTED:** When your prep inspection record proves polybag seal and barcode compliance before handover, we generate a formal claim package backed by exact timestamps, operator IDs, and photographic references.
2. **SUPPORTED:** When internal logs indicate genuine warehouse non-compliance, the agent refuses to claim, preventing fraudulent submissions.
3. **SILENT:** When evidence is missing, the agent explicitly returns \$0 recovery and documents the exact missing proof checklist, refusing to gamble your account health.
4. **UNCERTAIN:** When records are conflicting or ambiguous, we flag for human review rather than guessing.

In our baseline evaluation on 61 multi-tenant fee records, Recovery Manager achieved **100% claim precision**, recovering \$438.50 in baselessly charged defect and inventory fees while rejecting 49 invalid or ungrounded claims with complete audit traceability.

We are ready to deploy this directly into your existing ERP and warehouse stack with complete multi-tenant data isolation.

Sincerely,
