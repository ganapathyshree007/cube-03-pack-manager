# Customer Letter: Returns Manager

Dear Operations Leader,

Processing customer returns is one of the most operationally challenging steps in the fulfillment lifecycle. Every day, your facility receives returned parcels that require an immediate, accurate judgment: Is this the item we actually sold? Is it complete, or are crucial accessories missing? What condition is it in, and what should happen to it next?

Currently, operators must make these decisions manually, balancing the need for speed against the risk of incorrectly grading or misidentifying a product. Misgrading an item leads to margin loss—either by liquidating perfectly good restockable items or by unknowingly restocking defective or incorrect items that result in future dissatisfied customers.

**Returns Manager** transforms this process.

By combining structured visual inspection with your authoritative catalog and order data, Returns Manager provides an evidence-backed, consistent evaluation for every return. 

Here is how it helps:
- **Identity Verification**: It visually confirms whether the received item matches the expected SKU/ASIN based on catalog reference data.
- **Completeness Verification**: It checks the return against the expected parts list, explicitly identifying missing cables, manuals, or components.
- **Condition Assessment**: It evaluates the state of the product strictly against the authoritative condition scale, preventing ad-hoc or subjective grading.
- **Disposition**: It deterministically assigns the correct next step—whether to restock, refurbish, liquidate, or dispose—based on the evidence gathered.

Returns Manager is designed for the reality of operations. When evidence is blurry, missing, or contradictory, the system will output `UNCERTAIN` and instantly route the item for human review. It does not guess. Furthermore, every decision produces a structured, machine-readable evidence record, and authorized reviewers can seamlessly override judgments while the original AI decision remains preserved for auditing.

With Returns Manager, you will increase the accuracy of your return dispositions, recover lost margins by safely restocking more items, and empower your operators with a fast, structured, and fail-safe tool.

Sincerely,
The Returns Manager Team
